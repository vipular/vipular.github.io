# Audio, Speech and Language Processing: Lecture [H09K6a, H09K08a] (2026-2027 First semester)
- [KU Leuven page](https://onderwijsaanbod.kuleuven.be/syllabi/e/H09K5A)
- 6 ECTS credits
- Vipul Arora gives 1/3rd of the lectures. Rest of the course is taught by Prof. Hugo Van Hamme and Prof. Marc Moonen.

## LECTURES by Vipul Arora
### WHAT:
- 6 lectures of 2 hours each
- These are classical lectures in which we strive for a high degree of interaction
### CONTENT:
- Speech: Deep Neural Networks based Speech Recognition (Connectionist Temporal Classification, RNN Transducers, Unsupervised ASR and Confidence Estimation)
- Audio: Audio event detection, music analysis (melody estimation), audio retrieval
